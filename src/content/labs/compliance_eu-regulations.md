<h3>EU Data Regulations & Compliance Engineering</h3>
<p class="text-base sm:text-lg">
  Building compliant applications for the European Union requires technical guarantees baked directly into the system architecture, from data ingestion to storage and automated subject requests.
</p>
<ul>
  <li>
    <strong>Automated GDPR Subject Requests (DSAR):</strong> Programmatically fulfill Right to Access (Art. 15), Right to Erasure (Art. 17), and Right to Data Portability (Art. 20) with automated multi-database cleanup webhooks and immutable cryptographic audit logs.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>ePrivacy & Zero-Cookie Architecture:</strong> Differentiate strictly necessary operational storage (sessions, theme preferences) from consent-gated telemetry without using deceptive dark patterns.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Digital Markets Act (DMA) & Digital Services Act (DSA):</strong> Ensure open data interoperability, standardized export schemas, and transparent algorithmic recommendation disclosures.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>EU AI Act Article 50 Content Provenance:</strong> Machine-readable disclosure headers and watermarking flags for synthetically generated text, images, and audio.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Sovereign Cloud Data Residency:</strong> Route sensitive European personal data exclusively to EU-based data centers (e.g. Frankfurt, Paris) in compliance with Schrems II and cross-border transfer frameworks.
  </li>
</ul>
